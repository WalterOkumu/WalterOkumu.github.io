Scaling Multi-Country Platforms with Next.js and Customer Success in Mind

By Walter Okumu Oriaro Sep 17, 2025

In 2023, I led the migration of Yellow Pages Group’s platforms across 7 countries from Drupal to React/Next.js, tackling both SEO regressions and performance challenges.

The Challenge

Drupal platforms across multiple countries were slow, hard to maintain, and risked SEO collapse during migration.

The Solution

  • Architected Next.js infrastructure with server-side rendering for SEO.
  • Set up PostgreSQL + Node.js as the API backbone.
  • Implemented GitHub Actions + Docker CI/CD, cutting deployment times from hours to minutes.

Results

  • 99.8% uptime across 7 markets.
  • 30% organic traffic growth post-migration.
  • Deployment error reduction by 78%.
  • 40% CSAT improvement and 23% upsell growth tied to the new release cadence.

Lessons Learned

Success lies in aligning technical excellence with customer outcomes. SEO, uptime, and CI/CD aren’t just tech metrics — they’re business KPIs.
