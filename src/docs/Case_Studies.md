# Case Studies – Selected Projects

## Yellow Pages Group (Multi-Country Directories)
- **Countries**: Kenya, Tanzania, Cabo Verde, São Tomé, Angola, Mozambique, Timor Leste.  
- **Stack**: Next.js, Node.js, Tailwind, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, PM2.  
- **Impact**: Unified multi-country digital directories, boosting discoverability of SMBs.  
- **Results**: >200k businesses listed, improved uptime to 99.9%.

## Rhodium Digital Signage (www.rhodium.co.ke)
- **Description**: Replacing static media with digital signage solutions.  
- **Stack**: Raspberry Pi, Next.js dashboards.  
- **Impact**: Improved internal communications for clients like Momentum Credit.  
- **Results**: Scaled to national deployments, pilot with regional banks.

## Cura Global Recruitment
- **Description**: Global medical recruitment platform.  
- **Stack**: Next.js, Node.js backend.  
- **Impact**: Streamlined global hiring workflows.  
- **Results**: Expanded reach to multiple countries, faster candidate placements.

## Kua Wellness Africa
- **Description**: Employee wellness & recruitment services.  
- **Stack**: Next.js, Tailwind, API integrations.  
- **Impact**: Supported health + HR solutions for African enterprises.  
- **Results**: Growth in user adoption and program engagement.
