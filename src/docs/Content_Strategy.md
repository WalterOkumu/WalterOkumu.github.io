# Content Strategy – Portfolio Website

## Goals
- Establish Walter as a thought leader at the intersection of **Customer Success and Technical Engineering**.  
- Drive organic SEO traffic globally.  
- Provide long-form, high-quality insights.  
- Create trust and credibility.

## Content Categories
1. **Development & Engineering** – Next.js, Node.js, Tailwind, DevOps.  
2. **AI & Automation** – Agents, AI preparedness, business automation.  
3. **SEO & Marketing** – Digital visibility strategies.  
4. **Case Studies** – Deep dives into projects.  
5. **How-To Guides** – Practical tutorials.  
6. **Career** – Personal growth, thought leadership.

## Cadence
- **Phase 1**: 2 posts per week (first 3 months).  
- **Phase 2**: 1 post per week (sustained).

## Voice & Tone
- Professional yet approachable.  
- Explanatory and insightful.  
- Real-world problem solving.  
- Optimized for SEO intent (informational + transactional queries).

## SEO Focus
- Keywords: technical customer success, full-stack developer, AI automation, customer success engineer.  
- Metadata: 60-char titles, 160-char descriptions.  
- JSON-LD for blog posts.  
- Structured headings (H1, H2, H3).

## Distribution
- Website (MDX blog).  
- Cross-post summaries on LinkedIn, X, Dev.to.  
- Syndication to Medium/Hashnode.
